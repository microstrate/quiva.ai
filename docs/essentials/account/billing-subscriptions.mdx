QuivaWorks bills per account, not per person. A plan buys a monthly credit allowance and a set of storage and meeting allowances that everyone in the account shares. Payments are processed by Stripe.

## Where billing lives

Open **Billing & Plans** in the sidebar — it sits under **More**. Only the **root**, **admin** and **billing** roles can see it.

It has three entries:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Subscription Plan" icon="bell">
    Your current plan, **Change Plan**, and the card on file.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Plan & Balance" icon="bars-progress">
    Credit balance, usage, top-ups, and the credit usage log.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Billing" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square">
    Not a page — it opens the **Stripe customer portal in a new tab**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
Invoices, payment methods, tax details, cancellation and downgrade all live in the Stripe portal. There is no in-app billing history, payment-method form, tax form or cancel button.
</Info>

## Plans

The catalogue is **Free**, **Pro** and **Team**.

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | **$399 / month** | **$1,199 / month** |
| Seats | 2 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Included credits per month | 1,500 | 20,000 | 90,000 |
| Buy credit top-ups | No | Yes | Yes |
| Storage ceiling | 100 GB | 2,000 GB | 8,000 GB |
| Meeting recording hours | 5 | 20 | 60 |
| Meeting transcript hours | 5 | 20 | 60 |
| Conversation-history retention ceiling | 45 days | 90 days | 365 days |

<Note>
There is no per-user or per-seat rate. Paid plans are a flat monthly price with unlimited seats, so adding or removing people does not change what you pay.
</Note>

The retention ceiling is the longest you can set **Account → Data Retention** yourself. [Set your retention period →](/essentials/account/account-settings#data-retention)

### What counts as a seat

`Seats` counts **non-client** users. Clients sign in passwordlessly and consume no seat. **Suspended users do not occupy a seat** either, so suspending someone frees their seat without deleting them.

Every other role — admin, developer, monitor, billing and collaborator — occupies a seat alongside root.

## Credits

Credits are granted **per account, per month** and reset at renewal. Buying a top-up adds credits that **never expire**; purchased credits are spent last, after the month's included allowance.

The Free plan cannot buy top-ups — upgrading is the path.

### Top-up packs

Pro and Team are offered the same ladder:

| Pack | Credits | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 2,500 | $35 |
| Medium | 5,000 | $65 |
| Large | 10,000 | $120 |
| Bulk | 25,000 | $290 |

Buy a pack from **Plan & Balance → Buy credits**. The same section carries **Auto top-up** and a **Warn me at** threshold so you are not surprised by an empty balance.

### Tracking what you spend

**Plan & Balance** shows your Available, Included and Purchased balance, credits used this month, and a **Credit usage log** with a row per model call:

**Model · Date · User · Input Credits · Output Credits · Total Credits · Session ID**

You can **Refresh** it, **Export CSV**, or have the export emailed to you.

## Bring your own key

You can supply your own provider key for **Anthropic**, **OpenAI** or **Gemini**, or point at a custom endpoint. Keys are set per account.

<Warning>
Bringing your own key removes the token cost from a call. It does not make the call free — the platform component is still charged in credits.
</Warning>

## Changing your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription Plan">
    Open **Billing & Plans** in the sidebar, then **Subscription Plan**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Change Plan">
    Click **Change Plan** and pick the plan you want.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Complete checkout. Your new allowances apply to the account, not to individual users.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Downgrading to Free caps the account at **2 seats**. If you have more than two non-client users, remove or suspend people until you are within the cap before the downgrade takes effect.
</Warning>

To cancel a subscription entirely, use the **Billing** entry and manage it in the Stripe portal.

<Tip>
Closing the account is a different action, and it also cancels the subscription. See [Closing your account →](/essentials/account/closing-account)
</Tip>

## Invoices, cards and tax details

All of these are in the Stripe customer portal:

1. Open **Billing & Plans** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Billing**. A new tab opens on the Stripe portal.
3. From there you can download invoices and receipts, update or replace your card, and enter tax details.

<Info>
Card details are handled entirely by Stripe. QuivaWorks never stores your full card number.
</Info>

## Who receives billing email

Billing email goes to the **root user plus everyone holding the admin or billing role**. Suspended users and unaccepted invitations are skipped.

Adding a billing contact is therefore a role assignment, not a support request — give the person the **billing** role and they will start receiving billing mail and gain access to **Billing & Plans**. [About roles →](/essentials/users/roles-permissions)

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A payment failed">
    Open **Billing & Plans → Billing** to reach the Stripe portal and update the card. Common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, or a billing address that does not match the card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't see Billing & Plans">
    The entry is visible to the root, admin and billing roles only. Ask an admin to give you the **billing** role.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A charge I didn't expect">
    Check whether a credit top-up was bought, or the plan was changed, during the period. **Plan & Balance → Credit usage log** shows every model call with the user who made it, and the invoice itself is in the Stripe portal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invoices need different company details">
    Update **Company/Entity name**, **Address** and **Country** in **Account → Details**, and your tax details in the Stripe portal. [Account details →](/essentials/account/account-settings)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
