Creating a QuivaWorks account takes a few minutes: fill in the signup form, verify your email, and choose where your data is hosted.

## Before you begin

Have ready:

- An email address to own the account
- A unique account name — this is the identifier people sign in with
- The country or region your business is registered in

## Step 1: Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the signup page">
    Go to [https://app.quiva.ai/en/signup](https://app.quiva.ai/en/signup).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    - **Account Name (Unique Identifier)** — a unique identifier for the account, for example `acme-ops`. Minimum two characters.
    - **Owner Email** — your master login. It must be globally unique.
    - **Password**
    - **Country** — where the business is registered, or where you work from if you operate as an individual.
    - **Name** — first and last name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    Tick the box to accept the [Terms of Service](https://quiva.ai/legal.html#terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://quiva.ai/legal.html#privacy).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pass the human check">
    A Cloudflare Turnstile check runs on the form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### About account names

The account name works like an account ID. It is what lets the same email address be used across several QuivaWorks accounts — each one needs its own unique name, and members use it when signing in to your account specifically.

<Note>
Account names are **case-insensitive**. They are lowercased and trimmed when stored, so `AcmeOps` and `acmeops` are the same name and cannot both be taken.
</Note>

As the owner, you sign in with your email and password alone; you do not need to type the account name.

### Password requirements

Your password must be:

- At least **8** characters and at most **72**
- Both upper and lower case letters
- At least one number
- At least one special character

Common words are rejected. A password manager is the easiest way to satisfy all of this at once.

## Step 2: Verify your email

QuivaWorks emails you a confirmation. You can use either half of it.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Verification code">

    2. Find the 6-digit code.
    3. Enter it on the verification screen.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Magic link">
    1. Open the "Confirm Your Email Address" email.
    2. Click the confirmation button.
    3. You are signed in to your new account.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

</Info>

## Step 3: Choose how your data is hosted

The first time you sign in, an account with no hosting set is shown the screen **"Choose the way your data is hosted"**. This is not gated on your plan.

You choose two things:

- **Number of mesh nodes** — either **1** or **3**. More nodes mean automatic backups across multiple servers, traffic spread across them, and data kept in step across locations.
- **Region** (or **Replica regions** if you picked 3) — the region your data is held in.

Click **Save and continue**.

<Warning>
Adding regions increases storage usage proportionally. One region is standard usage; **3 regions is 3× the storage usage**.
</Warning>

## Step 4: Secure your account

After signup you are offered multi-factor authentication. It is worth doing straight away, though you can choose **Set Up Later** and come back to it.

1. Pick a method:
   - **Passkey** — sign in with touch, facial recognition, a device password or a PIN.
   - **Authenticator app** — a time-based code from an app such as Google Authenticator or Authy.
2. Follow the setup steps.
3. **Save your recovery codes.** You get 10 of them, and they are what gets you back in if you lose your device.

Store the codes in a password manager or somewhere else you control. You can view them and issue a fresh set later from **Settings → Password and authentication**.

[Authentication guide →](/essentials/security/authentication)

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The verification email hasn't arrived">
    - Check your spam or junk folder.
    - Give it a few minutes.
    - Confirm you typed the address correctly — the code goes to the **Owner Email** on the form.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="That account name is already taken">
    Account names must be unique across all QuivaWorks accounts, and they are matched case-insensitively, so changing only the capitalisation will not help. Try a different identifier — your team or product name with a suffix usually works.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The password is rejected">
    It needs 8 to 72 characters, upper and lower case, a number and a special character, and it must not be a common word. Generating one in a password manager avoids all four problems.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The human check keeps failing">
    Reload the page, disable browser extensions for a moment, make sure JavaScript is enabled, or try a different browser.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Using one email for several accounts

The same email address can own or belong to several QuivaWorks accounts, each under its own account name. To move between them, sign out, then sign in with the **account name** of the one you want. Each account has its own resources, billing and users.
