---
title: "Closing Your Account"
description: "How to close a QuivaWorks account, immediately or on a 14-day schedule"
icon: "circle-xmark"
---

Closing an account removes it and everything in it. Only the **root** user can do it.

You are offered two options: close **immediately**, or **schedule the closure 14 days out**. A scheduled closure can be cancelled at any point before it runs; an immediate one cannot.

<Warning>
Closing the account also cancels your subscription.
</Warning>

## Before you close

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Export what you need" icon="download">
    Download files, records and anything else you want to keep.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Disconnect integrations" icon="plug">
    Remove API keys from anything that uses them and disable webhooks pointing at the account.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Tell your team" icon="users">
    Everyone in the account loses access when the closure runs.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Consider downgrading instead" icon="arrow-down">
    Moving to the Free plan keeps the account and its data. [Change plan →](/essentials/account/billing-subscriptions#changing-your-plan)
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
The Free plan is capped at two seats. If you are downgrading rather than closing, suspend or remove people until you are within the cap — suspended users do not occupy a seat.
</Note>

## Closing the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account → Details">
    Open **Account** in the sidebar — depending on your account it sits at the top level or under **More** — and stay on the **Details** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Close Account">
    The **Close Account** button is at the top of the **Account Details** card. It is only shown to the root user, and it disappears once a deletion is already scheduled.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request a confirmation code">
    The **Select delete option** modal asks: *Do you want to delete the account immediately or schedule deletion in 14 days?* Click **Send code** and check your email.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the code and choose">
    Enter the code, then click either **Immediately** or **Schedule in 14 days**. Both buttons stay disabled until a code is entered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
There is no password re-entry step. The emailed code is the confirmation. If it does not arrive, you can request another once the countdown on the **Send code** button finishes.
</Info>

## Cancelling a scheduled closure

While a closure is scheduled, the root user sees a warning banner across the top of the app giving the deletion date and how far away it is, with a **Cancel account deletion** button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Cancel account deletion">
    The button is in the banner, on any page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm when prompted. The schedule is dropped and the account carries on as normal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the schedule is cancelled, **Close Account** reappears in **Account → Details**.

<Warning>
Choosing **Immediately** starts the closure straight away. There is no banner and nothing to cancel.
</Warning>

## What closure does

When the closure runs:

- The subscription is cancelled.
- The account and its resources are deleted.
- Every member's sessions are revoked and their membership of the account is removed.
- If the owner belongs to no other QuivaWorks account, their user record is deleted too.

If you or your colleagues belong to other QuivaWorks accounts, those are unaffected — each account is independent.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I get the account back after it closes?">
    No. Closure is not reversible once it has run. The window to change your mind is the 14-day schedule, and only if you chose that option.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can an admin close the account?">
    No. **Close Account** is shown to the root user only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to people who are in other accounts too?">
    Only their membership of this account ends. Their access to any other QuivaWorks account is unaffected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an alternative to closing?">
    Downgrading to the Free plan keeps the account and its data at no monthly cost. Suspending users keeps their accounts intact and frees their seats.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
