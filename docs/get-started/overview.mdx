QuivaWorks is a platform for getting work done with AI. **Abbie** is the AI coworker at the centre of it: you brief her in chat, she works through the task, and you can follow every step. Around Abbie sit **Spaces** for the work itself, **assistants** you configure for specific jobs, and **flows** that run automatically.

## What assistants give you

An assistant is configured across four areas, so it starts every conversation already knowing your context:

- **Instructions** — role, tone and responsibilities
- **Knowledge** — your documentation and reference material
- **Integrations** — your systems, connected over the Model Context Protocol (MCP)
- **Context Variables** — environment-specific parameters

Team assistants are available to everyone in the account; personal assistants are private to you. Account-level **Global Assistant Instructions** and **Global Assistant Knowledge** apply on top, on each assistant where you enable them.

## Creating your first assistant

Open **Assistants** in the sidebar. The create control offers three routes:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create with AI">
    Describe what you need and have the assistant configured for you. New assistants are created with Claude Haiku 4.5 as the model.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clone Assistant">
    Duplicate an existing assistant and adjust it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add from Marketplace">
    Install a pre-built assistant and make it yours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then set Instructions, add Knowledge, connect Integrations, and refine as you go.

## Working together

Any Abbie conversation can be shared — with named people as **Editor** or **Read-only**, or with everyone in the account. Access is managed by the conversation's owner and can be changed or revoked at any time.

## Plans

There are three plans: **Free**, **Pro** and **Team**. Pricing is per account, not per user, and paid plans have no seat limit. AI usage is metered in credits, which are included with your plan each month and reset at renewal.
