A conversation starts private to you. When you want other people in it, you share it — either with named people, or with everyone in the account. You decide who can reply and who can only read, and you can change or withdraw either at any time.

## Sharing a conversation

Open the conversation and use the **Share this conversation** control at the top of it, or pick **Share…** from the conversation's menu in the sidebar. Either opens the **Share conversation** panel, where all access to that conversation is managed. It is owner-only.

### General access

Three options, one of which is always in effect:

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Only me** | Nobody else can open this conversation. |
| **Specific people** | Only the people listed below. |
| **Everyone in the account** | Anyone in your account can read the whole conversation. |

Switching to **Everyone in the account** asks you to confirm, and tells you the whole conversation becomes readable — including everything already said. You can undo it at any time.

Switching to **Only me** also removes access for everyone you've shared it with, and says how many people that is before it does.

### Adding someone

Use the **Add someone** picker and set **as** to the access level before you pick the person — choosing the person is what sends the invite.

- **Editor** — can reply in the conversation
- **Read-only** — can follow the conversation but not reply

<Info>
Anyone mentioned in a message is added here too, as an Editor.
</Info>

### Changing and removing access

Every person on the list has their own access-level dropdown and a **Remove** button. Sharing is reversible: change someone from Editor to Read-only, remove one person, or switch general access back to **Only me** to revoke everyone at once.

<Note>
If the panel says "Couldn't load who's invited. This isn't a confirmed empty list.", the list failed to load — it does not mean nobody has access. Retry before assuming the conversation is private.
</Note>

### Copy link

The panel shows the conversation's link and a **Copy link** button. The link doesn't grant access on its own — while general access is **Only me**, the panel warns that only you can open it, and you need to share with specific people or the whole account first.

## Finding shared conversations

The conversation sidebar splits your conversations into three tabs:

- **Personal** — your own conversations
- **Team** — conversations shared with everyone in the account
- **Shared** — conversations other people have shared with you

## @mentions

Type `@` in a message to bring a teammate in. They're added to the conversation as an **Editor** and appear on the share panel's list, where their access can be changed or removed like anyone else's.

Keep mentions purposeful: say what you need from the person, and give them enough context to act without reading the whole thread.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I undo sharing a conversation?">
    Yes. Remove one person with the **Remove** button next to their name, or switch general access to **Only me** to revoke everyone at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone I share with reply?">
    Only if you gave them **Editor**. **Read-only** people can follow the conversation but not reply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can manage access?">
    The conversation's owner. The share panel is owner-only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I share with someone outside my account?">
    No. The picker offers the users in your account, so anyone you share with has to be a user there first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a shared conversation cost more?">
    Credits are consumed by the account, not by the person who happens to be typing, and are pooled monthly across the whole account. See [Plans & Pricing](/get-started/plans-and-pricing#credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will I be emailed when something happens?">
    Not for Abbie notifications — the notification settings say email isn't available for them yet. You get them in the app; open **Settings** from the user menu and go to **Notifications** to choose which ones.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
