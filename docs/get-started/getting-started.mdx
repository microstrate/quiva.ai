## Create your account

### Registration

1. Visit [https://app.quiva.ai/en/signup](https://app.quiva.ai/en/signup)
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Account Name (Unique Identifier)** — the name you and your team will sign in with. It is case-insensitive: it's lowercased and trimmed when saved, so `Acme` and `acme` are the same account. The same email address can be used across more than one account.
   - **Owner Email** — the account owner's email address. It can be changed later in personal **Settings** under **Credentials**.
   - **Password** — see the policy below
   - **Country**
   - **Name** — first and last
3. Accept the [Terms](https://quiva.ai/legal.html#terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://quiva.ai/legal.html#privacy)
4. Complete the Cloudflare check — to confirm you're a human

### Password policy

Your password must:

- be **at least 8 characters** and **no more than 72**
- contain at least one **uppercase** letter
- contain at least one **lowercase** letter
- contain at least one **number**
- contain at least one **special character**
- avoid **common words** — a password containing one is rejected

### Email verification

After registering, verify your email using either method in the "Confirm Your Email Address" email:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Verification code">
    1. Open the email
    2. Find the 6-digit verification code
    3. Enter it on the verification screen
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Magic link">
    1. Open the email
    2. Click **Confirm your email**
    3. You'll be taken to your new account
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
Verification links and codes expire after **2 days**. Your account is not deleted if you don't verify in time — a new verification email can be issued. Check your spam folder if nothing arrives within a few minutes.
</Note>

## Secure your account

We strongly recommend adding a second factor on your first login. You'll be offered it after verifying your email, and you can choose **Set Up Later** and come back to it in personal **Settings** under **Password and authentication**.

1. Choose a method:
   - **Passkey** — sign in with touch, facial recognition, a device password or a PIN
   - **Authenticator App** — Google Authenticator, Authy, or similar
2. Save your **recovery codes** somewhere safe. You get 10, and they let you back in if you lose your device.

<Warning>
Recovery codes are the only way back in if you lose access to your second factor. Store them outside the account.
</Warning>

<Info>
More detail in the [Authentication guide](/essentials/security/authentication).
</Info>
