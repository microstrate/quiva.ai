There are three plans: **Free**, **Pro** and **Team**.

<Info>
**Pricing is per account, not per user.** Pro and Team have no seat limit — the price is the same whether five people use the account or fifty. There is no per-user charge and no per-seat add-on.
</Info>

## Plan comparison

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0 | **$399 / month** | **$1,199 / month** |
| **Seats** | 2 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Included credits per month** | 1,500 | 20,000 | 90,000 |
| **Buy credit top-ups** | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Storage ceiling** | 100 GB | 2,000 GB | 8,000 GB |
| **Meeting recording hours per month** | 5 | 20 | 60 |
| **Meeting transcript hours per month** | 5 | 20 | 60 |
| **Conversation retention ceiling** | 45 days | 90 days | 365 days |

Seats count **non-client** users. The **client** role is passwordless, scoped to the resources granted to it, and consumes no seat. Suspended users don't occupy a seat either.

## Free

$0. Two seats and **1,500 credits a month**, with a 100 GB storage ceiling, 5 hours each of meeting recording and transcription, and conversation history kept for up to 45 days.

Credit top-ups can't be bought on Free — when you need more, the path is Pro.

## Pro

**$399 a month**, unlimited seats, **20,000 credits a month**. Storage ceiling 2,000 GB, 20 hours each of meeting recording and transcription, and conversation history kept for up to 90 days. Top-up packs can be bought at any time.

## Team

**$1,199 a month**, unlimited seats, **90,000 credits a month**. Storage ceiling 8,000 GB, 60 hours each of meeting recording and transcription, and conversation history kept for up to 365 days. Top-up packs can be bought at any time.

## Credits

Credits are how AI usage is metered. They are held **per account, per month** — not per user — and the included allowance resets at renewal.

### Top-up packs

Pro and Team can buy extra credits. The ladder is the same on both plans:

| Pack | Credits | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 2,500 | $35 |
| Medium | 5,000 | $65 |
| Large | 10,000 | $120 |
| Bulk | 25,000 | $290 |

Purchased credits **never expire** and are spent last, after the month's included allowance. Included credits do not roll over — they reset at renewal.

### Where to manage credits

Open **Billing & Plans** in the sidebar (it sits under **More**) and go to **Plan & Balance**. There you'll find:

- **Balance** — available, included and purchased credits
- **Credits used this month**, and what a credit buys
- **Buy credits**, **Auto top-up** and **Warn me at**
- **Storage & meetings** — storage GB-months, recording hours and transcript hours used
- **Credit usage log** — model, date, user, input/output/total credits and session ID, with CSV and email export

**Billing & Plans** is visible to the root, admin and billing roles.

## Storage and meetings

Each plan has a **storage ceiling**. It is a hard cap: writes are refused once you reach it, so it's set well above ordinary use rather than as a pricing lever. Usage is shown under **Plan & Balance → Storage & meetings**.

Meeting **recording** and **transcript** hours are counted separately, because a meeting that's recorded but not transcribed only draws on the recording allowance.

## Conversation retention

Your account's retention period controls how long assistant conversation history is kept before it's permanently deleted. Set it under **Account → Data Retention**. You can choose any period up to your plan's ceiling — 45 days on Free, 90 on Pro, 365 on Team.

Pipeline execution traces and diagnostics are separate and are always kept for **7 days**, whatever you set here.

## Bring your own key

You can supply your own model provider keys. Providers with active support are **Anthropic**, **OpenAI** and **Gemini**, plus custom endpoints. BYOK is configured per account.

<Note>
Bringing your own key removes the token cost from a call — you're billed for those tokens by the provider instead. The platform component of the charge still applies, so a BYOK call is cheaper in credits, not free.
</Note>

## Where your data is hosted

New accounts are asked to **choose the way your data is hosted** before they start. Today that is a single region — **Europe** — and a choice of **1 or 3** nodes. Running across 3 regions uses 3× the storage. The choice is not tied to your plan.

## Billing and payments

**Billing & Plans** has three entries:

- **Subscription Plan** — your current plan, **Change Plan**, your saved card and **Manage payment methods**
- **Plan & Balance** — credits, usage and top-ups, as above
- **Billing** — opens the Stripe customer portal in a new tab

Invoices, payment methods, tax details, cancellation and downgrades are all handled in the Stripe portal.

Billing email goes to the account root plus everyone holding the **admin** or **billing** role. Adding a billing contact is a role assignment, not a support request.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is pricing per user?">
    No. Pro and Team are a flat monthly price for the account, with no seat limit. Adding people doesn't change the bill.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    On Pro and Team, buy a top-up pack from **Plan & Balance**, or turn on **Auto top-up** so it happens automatically. Set **Warn me at** to be told before you get there. On Free, top-ups aren't available — moving to Pro is the path.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over?">
    Included credits reset at renewal. Purchased credits never expire and are spent only after the month's included allowance is used up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do clients and suspended users use a seat?">
    No. Seats count non-client users, and a suspended user doesn't occupy one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I keep conversation history for longer than my plan's ceiling?">
    Not on your own — the ceiling is 45 days on Free, 90 on Pro and 365 on Team. Ask through the [Help Centre](https://quiva.ai/help-center/) if you need something different.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans?">
    Yes — use **Change Plan** under **Subscription Plan**. Cancellation and downgrade are handled in the Stripe portal, reached from the **Billing** entry.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    The Free plan is permanent and needs no card. It includes 2 seats and 1,500 credits a month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Card payments, processed through Stripe. Payment methods are managed in the Stripe customer portal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
