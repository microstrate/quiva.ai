Abbie can work in Microsoft Teams: in channels, in group chats and in 1:1 chats, the same as she
does in [Slack](/abbie/connecting/slack) and in the web chat.

<Warning>
**Teams setup is not a single button.** Granting Microsoft 365 admin consent only approves Abbie
for your organisation — it does **not** put her in Teams. The app package still has to be uploaded
to your Teams app catalog and then added to the teams you want her in. Until that happens, Abbie
appears nowhere in Teams, however green the connection looks in QuivaWorks.

This is the single most common reason for "I connected it but nothing happens".
</Warning>

## What you need before you start

- A **QuivaWorks account admin** to start the connection.
- A **Microsoft 365 or Teams administrator** for the middle of it. If that is not you, send them
  the setup page — steps 2 to 5 are theirs.

## Connecting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve Abbie for your organisation">
    An account admin selects **Connect Microsoft Teams** from the **Where Abbie works** card on the
    [Abbie home page](/abbie/getting-started/abbie-home), or from Chat platforms in Integrations
    settings. That opens Microsoft's admin-consent screen.

    Approving it provisions Abbie in your tenant. The connection now shows as **consent granted** —
    not as installed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Abbie for Teams">
    From **Set up Teams**, download the app package. This is the file your Teams admin uploads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your Teams admin center">
    The setup screen links straight to the Manage apps page in the Teams admin center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Upload the package you downloaded. This puts Abbie in your organisation's Teams app catalog.

    QuivaWorks checks the catalog for you and moves on once it can see her there. **Check again**
    re-runs the check if it has not caught up yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which teams Abbie joins">
    Pick the teams to install her into — up to **30 at a time**. QuivaWorks reports the result for
    each one, and anything that failed stays selected so it can be retried.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your colleagues">
    Once Abbie is in the catalog, invite the people who should have their own Abbie in Teams. Each
    person's Teams identity is linked to their QuivaWorks account, so Abbie knows who she is
    talking to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Installing Abbie into a team **does not require the team's owner** — she is added directly. The
team list also includes private teams you may not be a member of. Choose deliberately.
</Note>

An install into a team covers that team's standard channels; you do not need to add her channel by
channel.

## Linking your own account

Each person links their Teams identity to their QuivaWorks account so Abbie knows who she is
speaking to. Message Abbie in Teams and she sends a link card, or link from the Chat platforms
section of Integrations settings.

Unlike Slack, there is no "DM her a keyword" shortcut — the card she sends carries its own button.

## If she stops responding

If Abbie says an administrator needs to approve her again, the tenant's consent is missing or
incomplete. Every surface that reports this offers a **Re-approve permissions** action, which
re-runs the admin-consent step from step 1.

## Managing the connection

From **Chat platforms** in Integrations settings you can connect another tenant, unlink your own
Teams account (which detaches your identity but leaves the app installed), or add more people.

Removing Abbie from a team, or from the tenant's app catalog, is done from Teams' own admin
surfaces.
