Connecting Abbie to Slack means she answers where your team already talks, in channels, in threads, and in DMs, not just inside QuivaWorks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Abbie into your workspace">
    An account admin selects **Connect Slack** from the **Where Abbie works** card on the [Abbie home page](/abbie/getting-started/abbie-home), or from the Chat platforms section of Integrations settings, and completes the Slack authorisation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Once installed, **Invite your team** (or **Add people** from settings) lets an admin bring specific people onto Abbie in that workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your own account">
    Each person needs their own Slack identity linked to their QuivaWorks account, so Abbie knows who she's talking to. This can happen automatically for whoever installed the app, or via:
    - A magic link Abbie DMs you: just message **link** to her in Slack.
    - Self-service linking, which matches your QuivaWorks email against your verified Slack profile address.
    - An admin invite, if someone sets this up on your behalf.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everyone you invite gets their own Abbie: their own conversations, their own backlog, their own rapport, the same as they would in the web chat.

## Talking to her in Slack

Message her directly in a DM, mention her in a channel, or reply in a thread she's already part of. Each thread keeps its own conversation with Abbie, matching how a session works in the web chat. Starting a new top-level message begins a new conversation, so keep replying in the thread to continue one.

## If Abbie joins a channel

When Abbie's bot is added to a channel, she'll ask for permission before reading any of that channel's recent history for context. If you say no, she simply starts fresh from that point on; nothing is read without that explicit yes.

## Managing the connection

From the **Chat platforms** section of Integrations settings, you can add another workspace, unlink your own Slack account (the app itself stays installed; this only detaches your identity from it), or invite more people. Removing Abbie from a workspace entirely is done from Slack's own app management.

<Note>
Slack is not the only option — Abbie also works in [Microsoft Teams](/abbie/connecting/teams).
Telegram is shown in the product as **Coming soon**; see [Coming soon](/abbie/reference/coming-soon).
</Note>
