The Abbie home page is your dashboard: a summary of everything Abbie is doing, with one click into each area. It's the first thing you see when you open Abbie, and it's greeted with a simple **Chat with Abbie** button to start a fresh conversation at any time.

Four cards make up the page, each loading and failing independently. If one is temporarily unavailable, the rest still work.

  <Card title="Where Abbie works" icon="comments">
    Which chat platforms Abbie is connected to. If your workspace has Slack installed, this shows it and lets you invite people or link your own account. See [Slack](/abbie/connecting/slack).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Abbie needs you" icon="inbox">
    Everything currently waiting on a decision from you, gathered in one place: backlog items she's parked, a rapport change she's proposing, and any unread messages from her that need a response. Nothing is dismissed from here directly; every row takes you to the place it's actually resolved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recent conversations" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Your last few chats with Abbie, including threads that started in Slack, so you can pick one up where you left off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="How Abbie is running" icon="sliders">
    Two account-wide dials: her model routing mode (cost, balanced, performance or accuracy) and whether she's allowed to reach out to you proactively. Everything finer-grained lives in [Abbie's settings](/abbie/configuring/settings-overview).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
Some cards show account-wide settings as read-only if you aren't an account admin, so you can see how Abbie is configured even if you can't change it.
</Note>

## "Abbie needs you" in detail

This card is worth understanding early, because it's the one place that gathers up everything that's genuinely blocked on you:

- **Parked backlog items**: something Abbie was working on that needs a decision, a missing piece of information, or ran out of room to keep going on its own. Opens straight into the [Backlog page](/abbie/backlog/backlog-page).
- **A proposed rapport change**: if Abbie's noticed a pattern in how you like to work and wants to adjust her personal calibration to you, it shows up here as a suggestion you can approve or ignore. See [Rapport](/abbie/teaching/rapport).
- **Unread messages from Abbie**: things like a check-in or a follow-up she's sent you. See [Proactive Abbie](/abbie/proactive/check-ins-and-follow-ups).

If nothing is waiting on you, the card simply says so.
