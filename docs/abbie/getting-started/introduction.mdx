Abbie is the AI assistant built into QuivaWorks. You chat with her the way you'd message a colleague, hand her things to get on with, and she keeps working on them even after you've closed the tab.

She isn't a search box or a one-off chatbot. She has a memory of who you are and how you like to work, a backlog of things she's tracking for you, and the judgement to come back and check in rather than waiting to be asked.

<Note>
**Abbie is not the same thing as an Assistant.** [Assistants](/assistants/overview) are bots you
build and configure yourself, each for a particular job. Abbie is the single coworker already built
into QuivaWorks — there is one of her, everybody gets their own, and you teach her rather than
configure her.
</Note>

## What Abbie can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Have a real conversation" icon="comments">
    Ask her questions, ask her to draft something, ask her to look into something: she reasons about the request before answering rather than just pattern-matching a reply.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep a backlog" icon="list-check">
    Ask her to do something and she can keep working on it in the background, picking it back up across conversations until it's done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Remember things" icon="brain">
    She holds onto facts about you and your organisation between conversations, so you don't have to repeat yourself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reach out first" icon="bell">
    With your permission, she can check in, follow up on things she's waiting on you for, and suggest ideas, on a cadence you control.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Work where you already do" icon="slack">
    Connect her to Slack and she answers in channels and threads, not just inside QuivaWorks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Learn new skills" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Teach her a new skill with instructions and reference files, or import one someone else wrote.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
