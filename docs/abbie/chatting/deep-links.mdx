Abbie's chat and cognition log both support deep links, so you can point a colleague, or your future self, straight at a specific moment rather than describing where to find it.

## Linking to a conversation

Every session has its own link. Opening a session from the sidebar, from recent conversations, or from a parked backlog item all take you to that same shareable URL.

## Linking to a specific step

The [cognition log](/abbie/transparency/cognition-log) goes one level deeper: a link can point at one particular run within a session, and even one particular stage within that run (classify, enrich, router, an individual expert, judge, verify, or output). Opening a link like this scrolls straight to that stage and expands it automatically, rather than leaving you to hunt through a long session.

This is what the **Open in cognition log** link on an expanded stage pill in the chat window points to. See [Watching her think](/abbie/chatting/watching-her-think).

<Note>
A session or run link only works for people who already have access to that conversation. Sharing a link doesn't grant anyone new access to your chats.
</Note>
