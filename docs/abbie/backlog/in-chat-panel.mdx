---
title: "The in-chat panel"
description: "Checking and managing your backlog without leaving the conversation."
icon: "comment-dots"
---

While you're chatting with Abbie, a badge in the header shows how many open backlog items belong to this session. Selecting it opens a panel over the conversation with those items and the same everyday actions you'd find on the [Backlog page](/abbie/backlog/backlog-page): marking something done, deferring a follow-up, or stopping an item, without navigating away.

The badge count refreshes automatically once a run finishes, so it stays current as Abbie adds to or completes items during the conversation.

For the complete management view, filtering, scheduling, promoting items, and seeing your team's backlog, use the [Backlog page](/abbie/backlog/backlog-page) instead.
