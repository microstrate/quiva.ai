---
title: "Suggestions"
description: "When Abbie proactively raises an idea, and how she avoids repeating herself."
icon: "lightbulb"
---

Beyond check-ins and follow-ups, Abbie can occasionally message you with a suggestion: an idea she's spotted that might be worth doing, based on what she knows about you and your account.

Suggestions are deliberately restrained:

- At most once a week.
- Only while you've been recently active; she won't surface a suggestion to someone who's gone quiet.
- Never alongside a check-in in the same pass, so you're not getting two proactive messages at once.
- She won't repeat a suggestion she's already made, even if it comes back to mind later.

Like check-ins and follow-ups, suggestions are their own toggle and can be turned off independently. See [Cadence and quiet hours](/abbie/proactive/cadence-and-quiet-hours).
