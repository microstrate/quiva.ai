Your **Personal profile** sits inside the Rapport tab, below your rapport settings. It is the
place for facts about you that do not fit into a preference field.

<Note>
This is yours alone. **No admin can read or edit it.** It is the personal counterpart to the
admin-managed [organisation profile](/abbie/configuring/organisation-profile).
</Note>

## What goes here, and what does not

Your rapport already holds the short structured preferences: name, tone, formality, answer length,
expertise, interests, language. The personal profile is for everything that needs a sentence.

| Field | For |
|---|---|
| **About me** | Your role and what you actually work on — *"Senior ops manager, mostly supplier contracts and renewals."* |
| **How I work** | Working style and any accessibility needs — *"I'm dyslexic — keep paragraphs short and use bullet points."* |
| **Constraints** | Your own always/never rules — *"Never send a quote without flagging assumptions."* |

## How it differs from the rest

Four surfaces shape how Abbie answers you, and they are easy to confuse:

| Surface | Holds | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| [Rapport](/abbie/teaching/rapport) | Tone, formality, length — short preferences | Yours |
| **Personal profile** | Prose facts about you | Yours, private |
| [Organisation profile](/abbie/configuring/organisation-profile) | Structured facts about the business | Everyone, admin-managed |
| [Corrections](/abbie/teaching/corrections) | "When X happens, do Y instead" rules | Yours |

<Tip>
Accessibility needs belong here rather than in a repeated instruction at the top of every message.
Written once, it applies to everything she sends you.
</Tip>
