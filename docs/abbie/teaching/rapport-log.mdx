**What Abbie's learned about you** is the audit trail for your rapport: a list of the changes that
have actually been applied, in order.

It is distinct from the drafts queue. The drafts panel shows what Abbie is *proposing* and waiting
for you to approve. The log shows what is *in effect* — including changes she applied on her own
because she was confident enough not to ask.

## Why it exists

Rapport shifts gradually. Without a log, "Abbie has started giving me much shorter answers" is
something you notice but cannot explain, and cannot reverse without hunting through the settings
guessing at which field moved.

The log makes each change legible: what changed, and when.

## Undoing a change

Every entry has an **Undo**. That reverts the individual change without touching anything else you
have set, and the reversion is itself recorded — so the log stays a complete account of how your
rapport reached its current state.

<Note>
Undoing an applied change is not the same as dismissing a draft. A dismissal declines something
that never took effect; an undo reverses something that did.
</Note>
