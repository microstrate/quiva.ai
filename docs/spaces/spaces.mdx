# Getting Started with Spaces

Welcome to Spaces! Whether you're managing a team project, organizing a creative workflow, or coordinating across departments, Spaces provides all the tools you need to keep everyone aligned and productive.

## What are Spaces?

Spaces is a comprehensive workspace management platform that brings together tasks, records, files, and team collaboration in one intuitive interface. Think of a Space as your digital headquarters—where your team plans, executes, and tracks everything that matters.

## Core Concepts

Before you dive in, let's cover the fundamental building blocks:

### Workspaces
Your workspace is the top-level container for all your work. Each workspace has its own set of users, permissions, and data. You can belong to multiple workspaces, each with different teams and projects.

### Folders
Folders help you organize your content hierarchically. You can create nested folder structures to match your organizational needs, whether that's by project, department, or client.

### Tasks
Tasks are your action items. They have titles, descriptions, due dates, assignees, and status. Use tasks to track work that needs to be done, monitor progress, and keep your team accountable.

### Records
Records are custom data types that you define. Unlike tasks, records are flexible—they can represent anything from client contacts to product inventory to meeting notes. You decide the fields and structure.

## Quick Start

### Create Your First Space

1. Click the **"Create Space"** button on your dashboard
2. Enter a name for your space (e.g., "Q4 Product Launch")
3. Optional: Add a description and choose a color
4. Click **Create**

Your new space is now ready to use!

### Invite Your Team

1. In your space, click **Settings** → **Members**
2. Click **Invite Members**
3. Enter email addresses (comma-separated for multiple people)
4. Choose their permission level:
   - **Admin**: Full access to settings and all content
   - **Editor**: Can create and modify tasks and records
   - **Viewer**: Read-only access
5. Click **Send Invitations**

Your team members will receive an email and can start collaborating immediately.

## Navigation Overview

### Main Sidebar
The left sidebar is your command center. Here you'll find:
- **Dashboard**: Overview of your space
- **Tasks**: Your task management hub
- **Records**: All your custom data types
- **Files**: Shared documents and attachments
- **Settings**: Space configuration and permissions

### Top Navigation Bar
Quick access to search, your profile, and notifications.

## Common Workflows

### Planning a Project
1. Create a **Task** for the main project
2. Break it into sub-tasks for individual team members
3. Set due dates and assign owners
4. Use the **Calendar View** to visualize deadlines
5. Create a **Record** type to track deliverables or milestones

### Managing Client Information
1. Create a **Record** type called "Clients" with fields like name, email, company
2. Add records for each client
3. Create **Tasks** linked to specific clients
4. Attach files and documents to record details
5. Assign team members as watchers

### Organizing Team Knowledge
1. Create a folder structure in **Files** that matches your team structure
2. Upload documents, templates, and guidelines
3. Use **Records** to create an internal directory or knowledge base
4. Link tasks and records to relevant files
5. Share specific files with relevant team members
